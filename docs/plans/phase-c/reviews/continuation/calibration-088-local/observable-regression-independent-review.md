## Semantic interpretation and limits

Approval is an intelligible human event, and publishing is observable behavior. A reader using everyday observability could imagine hearing “yes,” asking the lead, or observing a publication gate. Those possible observations are not identified evidence in this statement. Under the corpus's operational rule, inventing a record, gate, witness procedure, or retrospective inquiry would add the very mechanism whose absence makes this case negative. Naming an approver is not identifying a recorded approval artifact or a decision rule.

The current question spans two levels: broadly observable behavior, and sufficiently specified evidence for checking an approval-dependent condition. Its final broad observation instruction and positive behavior criterion can compete with its explicit missing-record exclusion. The distinction between `no` for no identified record and `unclear` for named but ambiguous evidence is also close enough to invite uncertainty. These are plausible explanations for low confidence, not explanations extracted from model reasoning: the raw records expose choices/probabilities, not rationales.

This is representational underspecification in the generic requirement, but it is not an unresolved oracle once the supplied operational rule is applied. No identified approval record is visible in the frozen state. The review cannot infer that approval is actually unrecorded in the world, nor that human approvals are inherently unobservable. A calibrated instrument must preserve that difference and must not turn every human approval into a negative case.

The bundles are not isolated observable-question experiments: faithful and single_constraint wording differs within the joint requests. Their results show persistence across those contexts, not a causal proof that observable wording alone caused the regression. No historical service/model baseline was independently inspected; “regression” is supported here as a current fixed-role acceptance failure, not as an independently established service-change diagnosis. The four supplements cover authorization and duty independence, not a positive recorded-approval contrast. This corpus therefore provides limited boundary coverage for approval observability and no proof of generalization.

## Permissible next decision

An independent tournament is justified as a falsifiable calibration experiment for this single role. Keep all other role questions fixed, retain the full frozen generic corpus and labels, preserve existing evidence and seal, and retain threshold 0.6 plus two confident agreeing confirmations. Freeze candidates and selection rules before any new answers, with no consumer-derived facts, confidence coaching, or case-specific answer cues. Score every generic cell and disclose any residual limitation. Any separately authorized additional generic boundary coverage should remain distinct from the immutable shipped oracle.

If candidates cannot clear that unchanged gate without narrowing the intended construct or exploiting this case, record an unresolved instrument/evaluator limitation rather than asserting success or changing labels. These results warrant testing a remedy; they do not warrant a seal, a counterfactual acceptance claim, or a readiness claim.
